**Name:** Helena Torres  
**Organisation:** 2AI - Applied Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (IPCA)

Helena Torres was contacted by email with the same broad expert-feedback questionnaire that was sent to Pedro Morais. No response had been received by the final thesis build, so no statements from Helena Torres are used as evidence in the main chapters.

The request covered the following themes:

- Semi-supervised learning and pseudo-labeling, including the 90% confidence threshold and the risk of confirmation bias.
- CNN architecture and image preprocessing choices for a lightweight edge-deployable classifier.
- Evaluation quality, including per-class metrics, confusion analysis, calibration and preprocessing consistency across devices.
- The use of Rust and Burn compared with a conventional Python/PyTorch workflow.
- Incremental learning, catastrophic forgetting and possible mitigation methods.
- Edge deployment, mobile-device latency, update distribution and future research directions.

This appendix is included for transparency about the external feedback process. The reflection and conclusion therefore distinguish between feedback that was actually received from Pedro Morais and feedback that remained pending from Helena Torres.
